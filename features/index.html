Shorts tab

Collections and media.

One tab, two feeds. Collections are the shelves; Media is the reels and photo posts that bring them to life.

  • 1
    Trending, New, For You

    Three collection feeds, plus server-side search across every public collection by name, hashtag or owner.

  • 2
    Reels and photo posts

    Vertical, snap-scrolling media with up to five photos or a 60-second video. Videos are transcoded to play on every phone.

  • 3
    Play ring

    A collection with tagged media shows a play button. Rainbow means unwatched; grey means you've seen it all. Play scopes the feed to that collection and drops you back when you're done.

  • 4
    Comments with replies

    Instagram-style single-level threads with @mentions, reply notifications and owner moderation.

Collections

A shelf you'd actually show someone.

The core of Shelfie. Individual or group, as structured as you want.

  • 1
    Sections and dividers

    Split a collection into named sections. Drag products between them; the layout persists.

  • 2
    Hashtags that colour themselves

    Type #skincare and it gets a stable pop colour everywhere it appears. Product tags and 'best for' tags share one field.

  • 3
    Privacy that means it

    Public, Followers or Only me, enforced in the database, not just the UI. Group collections are Members only by default.

  • 4
    Cover cropper and preview

    Pan and pinch to a 3:2 cover. Preview the feed card and the full page before you publish.

  • 5
    Likes, saves, comments

    Collections have their own counters, live-updated. Save someone's shelf to find it later.

Socials tab

Chat, follow, invite.

Realtime messaging built for sharing products, not just text.

  • 1
    Direct messages and groups

    Create a group from search, rename it, add or remove people, leave. Typing indicators and read cursors included.

  • 2
    Rich cards

    Share a collection, a reel or a product and it arrives as a tappable card with cover, title and counts.

  • 3
    Mute, block, report

    Mute a chat for 1h, 8h, 24h or forever. Block someone and they disappear from your feeds. Every report is reviewed by a human.

  • 4
    Invite codes and the follow graph

    Your username doubles as an invite code. See who joined because of you, follow back in one tap.

  • 5
    Push notifications

    Delivered only when you're not already looking at the thread, and never from a muted chat.

Products tab

A living catalogue, priced in CAD.

Browse, search, track launches and request anything we're missing.

  • 1
    1,300+ products, 300+ brands, 290 categories

    Searchable by name, brand or category, with a Recommended sort that falls back to what's popular this week.

  • 2
    Featured and Coming Soon

    Curated weekly. Tap the bell on a launch and get notified on release day. The release calendar shows the whole month.

  • 3
    Brand pages and buy buttons

    Every brand has a page. The buy button names the store ('Buy on Sephora'), so you always know where you're going.

  • 4
    Request by link

    Paste a store URL. Our sandboxed extractor reads the product name, brand, price and images, and an admin approves it, usually within a day.

  • 5
    Reviews with reactions

    Write a review, react with emoji, see what people with similar profiles think.

INCI tab

Ingredient lists, explained.

A rule-based analyzer that is fast, transparent and honest about uncertainty.

  • 1
    Three modes

    Paste a list, pick a product from the catalogue (1,000+ have ordered INCI), or compare two side by side.

  • 2
    Functions and flags

    Humectant, active, emollient, preservative, fragrance. Flags for common irritants and anything on your personal avoid list.

  • 3
    Concentration bands

    Derived from INCI ordering and the 1% line. Shown as ranges with a visible disclaimer, never as a fake decimal.

  • 4
    Canonical names

    Aqua / Water / Water-Aqua-Eau and Nylon-12 / Nylon 12 are matched as the same ingredient when comparing.

  • 5
    History

    Every analysis is saved and restorable. Delete any entry with a swipe.

Profile & personalisation

The app learns your shelf.

A beauty profile you fill in once, and a signature computed from what you actually collect.

  • 1
    Onboarding quiz

    Interests, goals, concerns, AM / PM, current routine (with an 'Other…' that only accepts real cosmetics terms), loved and avoided ingredients.

  • 2
    Signature brand and ingredient

    A weighting engine ranks the brands and ingredients across all your collections and shows your signature on your profile.

  • 3
    Three themes and foldable layouts

    Light, dark and Matcha. On a foldable, the tab bar moves to the rail and comments open beside the video.

  • 4
    Avatars and presets

    Upload, take a photo, or pick from an illustrated preset gallery.

  • 5
    Delete from inside the app

    Account deletion is a button, not an email. Notification and privacy preferences persist across devices.
